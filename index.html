How Overlap works

Compare cities and share a time that works.

  1. Add your cities. See local times and working hours together.
  2. Pick a time. Click or drag on the calendar. Connect your calendar to see existing events.
  3. Share it. Choose the recipient’s time zone under Saved times, then copy your availability.

Analytics privacy

When analytics is enabled on this site, you can choose whether to allow Google Analytics to measure visits and general actions such as adding a city, saving availability, connecting a calendar, or copying times. Google’s analytics script loads only after you allow it.

Our analytics events do not include calendar names, event titles, feed links, selected dates or times, copied text, or form values. Page URLs are sent without query strings or fragments, and referral links are reduced to their website origin. Google may also process standard browser, device, and network information as part of Analytics.

You can decline or change your choice through Analytics preferences in the planner footer. Declining keeps the planner available. Withdrawing consent clears this site’s analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop the analytics script. See Google’s privacy policy for information about Google’s processing.
